**Return window:** 30 days from the delivery date for a full refund to
the original payment method. Items must be unused and, where applicable,
in original packaging.

**Final sale:** Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout, gift cards, and
custom or made-to-order pieces (e.g. made-to-measure drapery) cannot be
returned.

**How a return works:** Once a return is started, we email a prepaid
return shipping label within 24 hours. Refunds are issued within 5
business days of the item arriving back at our warehouse; the customer's
bank may take a few additional days to post it.

**Damaged or incorrect items:** If an item arrives damaged or is not
what was ordered, it can be returned free of charge and outside the
normal 30-day window — ask for a photo of the issue if the customer
hasn't already sent one, and prioritize getting a replacement or refund
moving quickly rather than treating it like a standard return.

**Exchanges:** We don't process direct exchanges. The customer returns
the original item for a refund and places a new order for the item they
want instead.

**Large/freight items** (furniture): a return pickup is scheduled with
the freight carrier rather than a prepaid label. This can take longer to
arrange — set that expectation with the customer up front.
