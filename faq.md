**What does Aurora Home Goods sell?** Home textiles, ceramics, candles,
and a small assortment of solid-wood furniture, all designed in-house
and made from natural materials (linen, oak, stoneware, soy wax).

**Care instructions:** Linen textiles are machine washable on cold/gentle
and should be line-dried or tumble-dried on low. Ceramics are dishwasher
safe on the top rack but hand-washing is recommended for glazed pieces
with metallic detail. Wood furniture should be wiped with a dry or
barely-damp cloth — avoid all-purpose cleaning sprays, which can dull
the finish.

**Gift wrapping:** Available at checkout for $5 per item. A gift note
can be added at no extra charge.

**Price matching:** We don't price match other retailers, but we do
honor our own listed price if it drops within 7 days of a customer's
purchase — they can reach out to request the difference as store credit.

**Design consultations:** Free 30-minute virtual or in-store sessions
with a member of our design team, for help planning a room or choosing
between finishes. Easiest way to book one is right here in chat.

**Store hours (flagship store):** Monday–Saturday, 10am–6pm. Closed
Sundays and major US holidays.

**Loyalty program:** 1 point per $1 spent; 100 points = $5 off a future
order. Points are applied automatically at checkout once a customer is
signed in.
