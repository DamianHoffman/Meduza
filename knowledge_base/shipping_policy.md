**Domestic shipping (US):** Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard
shipping takes 4–6 business days and is free on orders over $75 (otherwise
$6.95). Expedited shipping (2–3 business days) is $16.95.

**Large/freight items** (furniture such as coffee tables, sofas, and
shelving) ship separately via a freight carrier and typically take
7–14 business days. A delivery window is emailed once the item leaves
the warehouse; a signature is not required.

**International shipping:** Currently available to Canada and the UK only.
Transit time is 7–14 business days. Customs duties, if any, are the
customer's responsibility and are not collected at checkout.

**Order tracking:** A tracking link is emailed as soon as a label is
created. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after that
email arrives.

**Address changes:** We can update a shipping address only before an
order enters "Processing." Once an order shows "Shipped," it can no
longer be redirected — the customer will need to work with the carrier
directly or arrange a return once it arrives.

**Delays:** If a package hasn't updated in 5+ business days, or an
estimated delivery date has passed by more than 2 days, treat it as a
possible lost shipment and offer to escalate to a human associate rather
than speculating about the cause.
